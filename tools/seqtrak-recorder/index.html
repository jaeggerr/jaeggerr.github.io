Capture mode

Sends MIDI Start, records the requested bars, sends Stop, then keeps Tail seconds so reverb and delay ring out. If the SEQTRAK sends MIDI clock, the bar count is read from it and the BPM field follows automatically.
Arms the recorder and waits. Press play on the SEQTRAK and capture starts on its MIDI Start; it stops when the machine does. Needs MIDI Clock output enabled on the device (Settings → MIDI).
You drive it: hit record, play the machine, hit stop. Works without MIDI — the only mode Safari can run.

Not seeing signal

Meters flat? The SEQTRAK routes audio over USB only while it is powered on and playing — check the machine's own volume too.

Mono input? macOS sometimes defaults the device to 1 channel. Open Audio MIDI Setup and set the SEQTRAK input to 2 ch, then rescan.

Nothing in the list? Some hubs hide USB audio. Try a direct USB-C cable.
